The room

One room, your group.

A long room running west to east, in two connected areas: a golden lounge with its arched bar, then a burgundy lounge under a string of bistro lights.

Both tables sit on the same axis: from the back, you see the whole room end to end.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How many people does the room hold?

About 34 seated guests across both tables (Table A ≈ 16, Table B ≈ 18). The exact capacity for your format is confirmed by the team when you book.

Can we book only part of the room?

Choose your preferred space in the form (golden lounge, burgundy lounge or the whole room): the team will tell you what’s possible for your date.

Does the room have its own bar?

Yes: an arched bar with a lit back bar and a full bartender station. Mention in your request if you’d like bar service during the event.

Can we give a presentation?

The room has a ceiling projector and a roll-down screen. Mention it in your request so the team can confirm you can use them.

What menu is served?

Barranco’s Peruvian cuisine. Note your preferences and allergies in the form: the team will reply with the options.

Can we bring a cake or decorations?

Mention it in your message: the team will confirm what’s possible.

Is there a minimum spend or deposit?

The conditions (hours, minimum, deposit) are sent to you with the availability confirmation.
